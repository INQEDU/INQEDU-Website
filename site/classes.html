Our specialism

English A

Our tutors specialise in both English A courses. This is where our approach to reading and argument began.

SLHL

English A: Language & Literature

This course pairs literary works with non-literary texts. One week you may study a novel, the next a political speech or an advertising campaign. The skill underneath is the same: explaining how a text is constructed to affect its audience.

  • Analysing non-literary text types: advertisements, speeches, articles, cartoons, websites
  • Reading for audience, purpose and context
  • Commenting on visual and structural features as well as language
  • Linking literary works and non-literary texts to a global issue for the Individual Oral
  • Planning and writing Paper 1 and Paper 2 under timed conditions
SLHL

English A: Literature

This course is the close study of literary works across poetry, prose, drama and literature in translation. It rewards students who can read slowly, notice precisely, and build an interpretation that is their own.

  • Close reading of unseen poetry and prose
  • Understanding form, structure and voice in each literary genre
  • Comparing works for Paper 2 without retelling the plot
  • Choosing extracts and a global issue for the Individual Oral
  • Developing a line of inquiry for the HL Essay
