History

SLHL

History rewards students who can weigh evidence and argue a case. We work on reading sources critically, planning essays that answer the question asked, and writing a historical investigation that stands up to scrutiny.

  • Using historical concepts and perspectives in an argument
  • Designing and writing the historical investigation (IA)
